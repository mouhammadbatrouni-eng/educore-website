Education

Everything teachers and academic leaders use every day: learning, early years, attendance, appointments, results, library, timetables and extracurricular activities.

EducoreLMSCurriculum, lessons, assignments and assessments.EducoreEYEarly years observations, learning objectives, journals and reports.EducoreIBInternational Baccalaureate (IB) curriculum support.EducoreAttendanceStudent attendance, absences, lateness and reports.EducoreAppointmentAppointment scheduling and coordination.EducoreAnalysisAcademic performance analysis and reports.EducoreLibraryBook lending and returns.EducoreDigitalLibraryDigital resources organised by categories, sub-categories and activities.EducoreTimetableClass, teacher and room timetable scheduling.EducoreECAExtracurricular activities, clubs and participation.

Operations

The daily running of the school: student records, enquiries and admissions, approvals, transport, dismissal, menus, withdrawals and reports.

EducoreSISStudent information, classes, registrations and school records.EducoreCRMLeads, enquiries, referrals and follow-ups.EducoreAdmissionApplications, admission tasks, documents and enrolment.EducoreWorkflowStaff requests, routing, approvals and status tracking.EducoreTransportTransport routes, bus assignments and transport updates.EducoreDismissalStudent pick-up/drop-off coordination and dismissal records.EducoreMenuSchool menu planning and publishing.EducoreWithdrawalStudent withdrawal requests, approvals and setup.EducoreReportCenterHR, admin, attendance, financial, VAT, accounting and academic reports in one place.

HR

Your staff from hiring to payroll: records, recruitment, payroll, evaluation and the staff team app.

EducoreHRMEmployee profiles, attendance, HR records and reports.EducoreRecruitmentPublish vacancies and receive applicant submissions.EducorePayrollPayroll periods, leave, deductions, loans and end-of-service calculations.EducoreEvaluationStaff evaluations and performance reports.EducoreAdminEvaluationEvaluation of administrative staff with KPIs and employee tracking.EducoreTeamsStaff communication and leave requests.

EducoreBillingInvoices, receipts, balances and statements.EducoreFinanceExpenses, accounts, journals, bank records and VAT reports.EducoreAssetsAsset management and tracking.EducoreInventoryInventory and stock records.EducorePaymentsPayment processing and integration.EducoreBudgetsBudgets and approval hierarchies.EducoreMarketplaceSchool products/services, purchases and related payments.EducoreExternalOperationsClients, invoices, receipts and cash sales for external customers.
