EducoreSIS

Student information, classes, registrations and school records.

  • School setup: campuses, grade levels, classrooms and schedules
  • Fee groups
  • Parents’ and children’s profiles with contacts and relationships
  • Registrations to classes and grade levels
  • Classes management: teachers, class sizes and progress
  • Admin reports on attendance, performance and finance
  • Light employee profiling
  • Registration periods
  • Student promotion and fast track
  • Assign elective subjects
  • Merge classes
  • Roles
  • Alumni students and media download

EducoreCRM

Leads, enquiries, referrals and follow-ups.

  • KYC and school tours (booked and taken)
  • No-enrollment reasons
  • Manage leads
  • Referrals and enquiry sources
  • Social media inquiries: Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, X and LinkedIn
  • Email templates and email automation
  • Mass mailing
  • Walk-ins and Educore Forms

EducoreAdmission

Applications, admission tasks, documents and enrolment.

  • Admission Q&A and admission form attachments
  • Manage admission applications
  • Add tasks to each application
  • Track required data and documents
  • Register students online and convert leads into registrations

EducoreWorkflow

Staff requests, routing, approvals and status tracking.

  • Staff groups
  • Request types: HR, inventory and more
  • Build approval workflows
  • Tracking dashboard for request status

EducoreTransport

Transport routes, bus assignments and transport updates.

  • Bus list report
  • Manage buses
  • Assign staff, trips and students
  • GPS tracking of buses (requires tracking hardware)
  • Parents notified on boarding and drop-off
  • Live bus tracking in EducoreParent

EducoreDismissal

Student pick-up/drop-off coordination and dismissal records.

  • Parents send pick-up/drop-off requests
  • The gate is notified automatically
  • Monitor requests live
  • Dismissal records
