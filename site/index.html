Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is your data with Educore?
Our Security Center includes cPHulk Brute Force Protection, Host Access Control, ModSecurity and a Security Adviser including ImunifyAV, plus Shell Fork Bomb Protection. Our security framework complies with GDPR, with perpetual security and DDoS protection. To detect and block attacks on our technical infrastructure, we process identification, connection or localization data under our legitimate interest in protective measures; this data is processed by Cloudflare Inc. under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Educore is also ISO 27001:2022 certified.
How are we different from other daycare platforms?
We are a childcare management software dedicated to providing better education and overall upbringing to your children. Managing a full-fledged school is not other platforms’ forte. With Educore, parents can raise concerns, pay fees, receive assessment reports for their kids and much more.
Does Educore provide training sessions to clients?
Our apps are built with intuitiveness at their core, even older guardians use them seamlessly. We also provide training and support during onboarding, along with additional material for further help.
Does Educore work across all platforms?
Yes. Educore runs on Web, iOS and Android across tablets, computers, laptops and smartphones, so you can access it anytime, anywhere, at a price that works for you.
Can payments & transactions be made online?
EducoreParent has an integrated payment system supporting all major payment portals. School owners can raise invoices, accept payments and generate automated, branded receipts in the app.
What do I need to get started with Educore?
All you need is an internet connection and a tablet or mobile phone for each room. We take care of the rest, we onboard all your data or migrate it from your existing system within a day.
